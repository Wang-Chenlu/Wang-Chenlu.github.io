Hi! I’m Chenlu Wang. I come from Xi’an, China, a city with a rich history as the capital of thirteen dynasties and plenty of good reasons to stay for another meal.

I received my Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering from the Institute of Process Engineering (IPE), Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS), in 2024, under the supervision of Prof. Hongyan He and Prof. Yanlei Wang. My research focuses on computational materials science. By combining density functional theory (DFT), molecular dynamics (MD) simulations, and force field development, I study complex molecular and ionic systems. I’m particularly interested in the molecular mechanisms relevant to CO₂ separation, electrochemical energy storage, and the properties of functional materials.
